Developers worry Bun's Anthropic acquisition will erode the runtime's engineering quality

OPEN SOURCE BUN ANTHROPIC
THE BRIEF

Anthropic acquired Bun in December 2025 and kept it MIT-licensed, but a widely-read post argues that as Bun's team integrates further into Anthropic, the product-layer decisions shaping Claude Code — reduced reasoning defaults, billing confusion, unusual content restrictions — may eventually affect Bun's developer experience too. The author is switching personal projects to pnpm and plans to reassess in a year.

CONTEXT

The concern is not about Bun's code quality today — the post explicitly praises the team — but about the trajectory. Claude Code's April 2026 issues (postmortem published, reports of commit-history-based content filtering) gave the author a concrete data point: Anthropic's product decisions have already visibly degraded one developer tool it controls.

The HN thread reached 460 points, suggesting the concern resonates broadly. Bun's team has not publicly responded.

WHAT TO WATCH

  • Watch Bun's release cadence and changelog over the next six months — frequency and depth of changes are the clearest signal of whether the team's focus has shifted.
  • Check whether any Bun features begin requiring Anthropic account sign-in or cloud connectivity — that would be the clearest enshittification signal.
  • Evaluate pnpm or alternative runtimes now if Bun is a single point of failure in your build pipeline — optionality is cheap, dependency on any one corporate-acquired tool carries non-zero risk.
