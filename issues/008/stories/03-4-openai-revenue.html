OpenAI reaches $3.4B monthly revenue — API and enterprise now outpace ChatGPT subscriptions

OpenAI's April 2026 revenue run rate hit $3.4B per month ($40.8B annualised), up from roughly $2B per month at the start of the year. For the first time, API and enterprise contract revenue is reported to exceed consumer ChatGPT subscriptions as the primary revenue driver — a mix shift that has material implications for margin and growth trajectory ahead of a potential IPO.

The figures, reported by Bloomberg citing internal documents, put OpenAI's annualised revenue at more than double the $20B figure cited at the start of 2026. The API and enterprise segment is growing faster because large enterprise deals — including Microsoft's expanded reseller agreement and reported contracts with several Fortune 100 companies for custom deployments — carry higher average selling prices and longer commitment terms than $20/month consumer subscriptions.

The mix shift matters because enterprise revenue is stickier and more predictable, which improves the business's credibility ahead of an IPO. It also signals that OpenAI's platform bet — getting developers to build on its API — is converting into durable revenue, not just a consumer usage spike tied to ChatGPT's viral adoption cycle.

What to watch

  • Watch whether Anthropic discloses a comparable revenue figure in the coming weeks — the $30B ARR figure cited in some reports would make the gap between the two companies narrower than their valuation gap suggests.
  • Check your organisation's OpenAI API spend trend against these growth numbers: if OpenAI's enterprise segment is growing faster than consumer, expect pricing pressure on API tokens to ease as they prioritise contract volume over per-call margin.
