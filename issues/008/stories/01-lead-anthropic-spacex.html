FILED · UNDER

INFRASTRUCTURE COMPUTE ANTHROPIC

Anthropic has secured the full compute capacity of SpaceX's Colossus 1 data center — more than 220,000 Nvidia GPUs and 300 megawatts of power — in a deal that doubles Claude Code rate limits within days and signals a détente with Elon Musk's AI ambitions.

¶ 01 WHAT · HAPPENED

Anthropic announced on May 6 that it will access all computing resources at SpaceX's Colossus 1 facility in Memphis, Tennessee, adding more than 220,000 Nvidia processors and 300 megawatts of new capacity within a month. The deal sits alongside other recent compute agreements with Amazon, Google, Microsoft, Nvidia, and Fluidstack, making it Anthropic's largest single infrastructure expansion to date. Financial terms were not disclosed. Claude Pro and Max subscribers see immediate benefits: Claude Code rate limits double, peak-hour usage caps are removed, and developers gain sharply increased API request volumes for Claude Opus models. Anthropic says the additional capacity will also expand availability in international markets serving healthcare and financial services.

300MW New compute capacity from Colossus 1 alone — equivalent to powering roughly 250,000 average US homes.
¶ 02 WHY · IT · MATTERS

The deal is notable on two levels. Commercially, it addresses Anthropic's most pressing constraint: Musk has repeatedly criticized Anthropic publicly, yet SpaceX agreed to supply the company with its most powerful compute cluster. Musk confirmed he met with senior Anthropic leadership and found them "highly competent." The arrangement suggests that infrastructure scarcity can override ideological friction in the current AI race. Technically, Colossus 1 is one of the densest GPU clusters in the world. Pairing 220,000 H-series Nvidia processors with 300 MW of power provides Anthropic room to train larger models and serve higher inference volumes simultaneously — a bottleneck that has caused Claude usage limits and rate restrictions for paying users.

"SpaceX could reclaim compute if Claude engages in harmful actions."

— ELON MUSK, ON TERMS OF THE COLOSSUS 1 DEAL

¶ 03 WHAT · TO · WATCH

Watch for Anthropic's next model release: the expanded compute suggests a training run of a larger model is likely in preparation, possibly targeting a mid-2026 launch to challenge GPT-5.5 and Gemini 3 Pro. Track whether Claude Max rate limits actually increase within 30 days as Anthropic promised — this is the most concrete, user-verifiable commitment in the announcement. Also watch whether the orbital data center collaboration with SpaceX moves beyond a press-release bullet point: if SpaceX's Starship reaches the cadence needed to orbit data-center hardware, Anthropic would gain compute that no competitor can physically touch.
