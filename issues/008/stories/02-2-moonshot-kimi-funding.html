FILED · UNDER

FUNDING CHINA AI LLM

Moonshot AI has raised $2B at a $20B+ valuation, accumulating $3.9B in six months to become China's most-funded large language model company — while its Kimi chatbot reaches $200M in annualized recurring revenue.

¶ 01 WHAT · HAPPENED

Moonshot AI closed a roughly $2 billion funding round on May 7, pushing its valuation past $20 billion. Meituan's venture arm Long-Z Fund led the round — contributing more than $200 million on its own — alongside China Mobile and private equity firm CPE. The round follows three earlier tranches completed in January and February totaling $1.9 billion, bringing Moonshot's cumulative six-month total to $3.9 billion, the highest among China's large language model startups. Kimi's annual recurring revenue crossed $200 million in April 2026, driven by subscriptions to its consumer chatbot and enterprise model API access. The company released Kimi K2.6 — a frontier-tier open-weights coding model — in late April, which was covered in Issue 006.

$3.9B raised by Moonshot AI in under six months — more than any other Chinese LLM company.
¶ 02 WHY · IT · MATTERS

China's AI funding is now running at a pace that directly challenges the assumption that US compute export controls have capped Chinese model capabilities. Moonshot is building frontier models despite restricted access to Nvidia's latest chips — it uses a combination of older Nvidia hardware, domestic Huawei Ascend processors, and aggressive model efficiency techniques. The $200M ARR figure is also significant: it shows Moonshot has moved past the pure-research phase and into recurring commercial revenue, which gives it a path to profitability that most Western AI labs have not yet demonstrated at comparable scale. Meituan's participation matters strategically: the food-delivery and local services giant has a distribution network of hundreds of millions of users who could integrate Kimi AI into daily transactions.

¶ 03 WHAT · TO · WATCH

Check whether Meituan integrates Kimi into its super-app within the next two quarters — that distribution deal would be the real payoff for Long-Z's investment. Evaluate Kimi K2.6's API pricing against GPT-5.5 and Claude Opus for your own workloads: Chinese frontier models have consistently undercut Western pricing by 60–80%. Ask whether your organization has a policy on using Chinese-hosted AI models given data-sovereignty concerns — the funding round brings Moonshot more state-adjacent capital (China Mobile is state-owned).
