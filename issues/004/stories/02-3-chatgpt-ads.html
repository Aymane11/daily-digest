AI BUSINESS ADVERTISING

ChatGPT's ad system decoded: a four-token Fernet attribution chain and in-app webview tracking

WED 29 APR 2026 · 3 MIN READ

TL;DR

A researcher reverse-engineered ChatGPT's ad attribution system and published a full technical breakdown. Ads arrive as single_advertiser_ad_unit objects injected into the response stream. Four encrypted Fernet tokens handle spam integrity, forward attribution (30-day cookie), impression tracking, and server-side reconciliation. Clicks open in ChatGPT's in-app webview, where OpenAI's OAIQ SDK reports product views back to bzr.openai.com.

¶01 · WHAT HAPPENED

A researcher with access to ChatGPT's ad-enabled experience intercepted and analysed the full ad delivery pipeline. The breakdown, published on buchodi.com, maps every component from ad selection through post-click merchant reporting.

The system uses contextual targeting tied to conversation topics. One user in the research sample received six different ads across six unrelated conversations (travel, entertainment, productivity). Ads surface as carousel cards with advertiser branding and encrypted tracking tokens embedded in the payload.

Critically, ad clicks do not open in an external browser. They load in ChatGPT's in-app webview, keeping users inside the OpenAI surface and allowing OpenAI to observe post-click behaviour—page views, product interactions—before the session ends.

¶02 · WHY IT MATTERS

The four-token architecture gives OpenAI a closed attribution loop: it knows which conversation topic generated the ad impression, which user clicked, which product page they viewed, and whether the merchant's OAIQ SDK fired a conversion event. This is behaviorally similar to the cookie-based attribution systems that drove Google and Meta's ad businesses—except it is grounded in conversation context rather than browsing history.

The in-app webview is the key structural choice. By keeping clicks inside ChatGPT, OpenAI avoids losing the user to Safari or Chrome, retains attribution signal, and can gate ad-free access to paid subscribers. This mirrors the path Facebook took with its in-app browser, which became a significant privacy and regulatory flashpoint in 2022.

¶03 · WHAT TO WATCH

Check whether your ChatGPT plan serves ads. Free-tier and some Plus users see ads; Pro subscribers currently do not. If you use ChatGPT for research workflows, evaluate whether contextual ad injection affects the neutrality of responses adjacent to sponsored content.

Track regulatory attention. The Fernet token design means OpenAI holds a durable attribution graph linking conversation topics to purchase intent. EU regulators have already flagged AI-native advertising as a gap in current DSA and GDPR enforcement. Watch for guidance from the EDPB in Q3 2026.

THE FOUR TOKENS

ads_spam_integrity_payload
Server-side fraud detection
oppref
Forward attribution stored in browser cookies for 30 days
olref
Impression and outbound link tracking
ad_data_token
Server-side reconciliation at click time
