OpenAI lands on AWS Bedrock—GPT-4.1, GPT-5.5, and o3 now available through Amazon's managed AI platform

→ The $50B AWS infrastructure deal produces its first commercial yield: three OpenAI models in Bedrock preview. One API, one IAM policy, no separate OpenAI account required.

In the immediate aftermath of the Microsoft–OpenAI restructuring, OpenAI has deployed its first models to AWS Bedrock—the managed AI platform used by roughly 100,000 AWS customers. GPT-4.1, GPT-5.5, and o3 are available in limited preview via standard boto3 calls with IAM authentication. The move converts OpenAI's $50B AWS infrastructure financing into a two-sided distribution deal: AWS gets OpenAI's most capable models alongside Anthropic's Claude and Amazon's Titan; OpenAI gains access to AWS's enterprise customer base without building its own enterprise sales motion. Pricing matches OpenAI direct: $5/$30 per million tokens for GPT-5.5.

Agentic AI World Modeling: A Survey of 400+ Works Across Four Law Regimes

42 authors synthesize the agentic AI literature into a three-level taxonomy (L1 reactive, L2 predictive, L3 causal) mapped across physics law, biological law, human law, and digital law regimes. The result is a diagnostic framework for evaluating where any agentic system fails to model the world correctly.

Practical takeaway: for any agent you deploy, identify which world-modeling level it operates at and which law regime it will encounter. Mismatches between the two are the primary source of unexpected agent failures.
