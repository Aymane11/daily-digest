AI MODELS XAI

xAI releases Grok 4.3 at rank 53 on the Intelligence Index with 37–58% price cuts

Input drops to $1.25 per million tokens and output to $2.50 — pricing the model below GPT-4o and well inside Gemini Flash territory on a capability-per-dollar basis.

BY · XAI TEAM · 2 MIN READ

★ WHAT HAPPENED

xAI released Grok 4.3 on April 30, dropping input pricing 37% to $1.25/M tokens and output pricing 58% to $2.50/M tokens compared to Grok 4.2. The model scores 53 on Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index, placing it in the same tier as Claude Sonnet 4.5 and above Gemini 1.5 Pro. Context window remains 131K tokens. The API release came alongside availability via the x.ai/api endpoint with no waitlist. xAI also confirmed Grok 4.3 is the same architecture as Grok 4.2 — the improvements are training recipe and RLHF changes rather than a parameter increase.

¶ 01 WHY · IT · MATTERS

The pricing move is significant because it positions a frontier-tier model at sub-$2 output pricing for the first time from a major lab. At $2.50/M output tokens, Grok 4.3 is cheaper than GPT-4o mini on output and meaningfully cheaper than Claude Haiku 3.5 on input. For high-volume inference tasks — document processing, code review at scale, batch summarisation — this changes the cost calculus substantially. The capability-per-dollar argument is the emerging battleground among the top three labs, and xAI's aggressive pricing forces competitors to respond.

¶ 02 WHAT · TO · WATCH

Run Grok 4.3 against your existing benchmark task — particularly any workloads you currently route to GPT-4o or Claude Sonnet — and compare quality and latency before switching to chase the lower price. Track whether Anthropic or OpenAI respond with a price drop within 30 days; the pattern from 2025 is that aggressive xAI pricing triggers within-month reductions from competitors. Note that xAI's terms restrict use for training competing models — check your use-case against the API terms of service before building fine-tuning pipelines on Grok outputs.
