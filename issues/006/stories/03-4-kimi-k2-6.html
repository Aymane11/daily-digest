AI MODELS OPEN WEIGHTS

Kimi K2.6 hits general availability — 1 trillion parameters, 58.6% on SWE-bench

Moonshot AI's mixture-of-experts model activates 32B parameters per forward pass, supports a 262K context window, and ties GPT-5.5 on coding benchmarks at a fraction of the inference cost.

BY · MOONSHOT AI · 2 MIN READ

★ WHAT HAPPENED

Moonshot AI released Kimi K2.6 to general availability. The model is a mixture-of-experts architecture with 1 trillion total parameters and 32 billion active parameters per forward pass — keeping inference costs comparable to a 32B dense model despite the larger total parameter count. The context window is 262K tokens. On SWE-bench Verified, K2.6 scores 58.6%, placing it level with GPT-5.5 on that benchmark. The model is also available as an open-weight release on Hugging Face under Moonshot's research license. Warp has added K2.6 to its "auto (open)" model routing alongside MiniMax and Qwen3.

¶ 01 WHY · IT · MATTERS

A 1T-parameter MoE model that activates only 32B per pass breaks the usual trade-off between capability and inference cost. The 262K context window comfortably handles full codebases, long legal documents, and multi-hour transcript processing without chunking. Matching GPT-5.5 on SWE-bench while being available as open weights means teams can self-host a frontier-tier coding model for the first time. The Hugging Face release also enables fine-tuning on proprietary data — previously only possible with Llama-class models that trade off some benchmark performance.

¶ 02 WHAT · TO · WATCH

Test K2.6 on your hardest coding tasks before committing to a self-hosted deployment — the 32B active parameter count means it runs comfortably on two A100s, but MoE routing overhead can add latency unpredictably on smaller batch sizes. Check the Moonshot research license before building a commercial product on the open-weight release; it restricts certain distribution scenarios. Watch whether the 58.6% SWE-bench score holds up on real-world agentic coding tasks, which often diverge significantly from curated benchmark suites.
