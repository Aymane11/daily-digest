ENTERPRISE SECURITY

Microsoft Agent 365 goes GA; its Shadow AI scanner finds 4,200 unsanctioned tools in first week

Microsoft’s enterprise agent platform is generally available with Copilot Studio integration, Azure AI Foundry backend, and a new Shadow AI discovery module that already surfaced thousands of unauthorized tools across early-access tenants.

BY · EDITORIAL TEAM · 2 MIN READ

★ WHAT HAPPENED

Microsoft Agent 365 is generally available as of May 20, built on Azure AI Foundry and deeply integrated with Copilot Studio for low-code agent authoring. The GA release ships a Shadow AI Discovery module — a passive scan of Microsoft 365 tenant traffic that identifies AI tools employees are using outside of IT approval. In the early-access rollout covering 120 enterprise tenants, the scanner flagged 4,200 distinct unsanctioned AI applications in the first seven days, with consumer ChatGPT, Claude.ai, and Perplexity topping the list.

¶ 01 WHY · IT · MATTERS

Shadow AI Discovery reframes the enterprise AI conversation: instead of arguing about whether employees are using unauthorized tools, IT can now see exactly which ones and at what volume. The data from 120 tenants suggests the unsanctioned AI problem is not a niche behaviour — it’s baseline. That shifts the compliance pressure significantly. Microsoft is also positioning Agent 365 as the channel to bring shadow use into a governed environment, which is a classic Microsoft enterprise playbook: discover the shadow, then offer the sanctioned replacement.

¶ 02 WHAT · TO · WATCH

If your organisation runs Microsoft 365, check whether Shadow AI Discovery activates automatically at GA or requires an explicit opt-in — the early-access notes suggest it defaults to passive monitoring only, with no employee notifications. Watch for the competing Shadow AI detection products from Zscaler and Netskope to update their marketing responses. And audit your own Copilot Studio licensing: Agent 365 agents consume compute from the same pool as Copilot, and GA pricing is not yet published.
