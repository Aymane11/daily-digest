05WHAT IT MEANS
3 TAKEAWAYS
01

OpenAI’s revenue diversification beyond subscriptions is now underway

ChatGPT’s 700M+ monthly users create an intent signal that advertisers are willing to pay for. The CPC model mirrors early Google AdWords dynamics: low barrier to entry, bidding drives price discovery over time.

02

The privacy architecture is aggregate-only — for now

OpenAI’s current architecture shares only aggregate impressions/clicks with advertisers, not conversation content. Watch for policy updates as the platform scales — the temptation to enrich targeting with intent signals will grow with revenue pressure.

03

Check if your current ChatGPT enterprise agreement addresses ad exposure

Enterprise and Team plan terms differ from consumer ChatGPT. Verify whether your agreement explicitly opts users out of ad exposure before the self-serve platform expands geographically.

§ 03 · WHAT TO WATCH

Evaluate whether your business should run a ChatGPT ad campaign while the market is nascent and CPCs are low. Check OpenAI’s advertiser terms for any clause that changes data-sharing rules as the platform scales. Watch for Google and Perplexity responses — if ChatGPT’s ad product proves effective, it will accelerate AI search monetisation industry-wide.
