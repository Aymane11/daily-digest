OpenAI hits 10GW AI compute target three years ahead of schedule

→ Stargate program adds 3GW in 90 days, surpasses original 2029 goal

OpenAI has secured contracts for 10 gigawatts of AI computing capacity in the United States, achieving a goal it originally targeted for 2029. The company announced the milestone Wednesday, saying it added over 3GW in the past 90 days alone. Stargate, the joint venture with Oracle and SoftBank, has evolved from a $500B initiative into the umbrella brand for all of OpenAI's data center expansion plans. The accelerated timeline reflects intensifying competition for AI infrastructure as the company prepares for next-generation model releases.
