INFRASTRUCTURE MILESTONE

OpenAI hits 10GW AI compute target three years ahead of schedule

Stargate program adds 3GW in 90 days, surpasses original 2029 goal

BY · BLOOMBERG · 4 MIN READ

★ WHAT HAPPENED

OpenAI has secured contracts for 10 gigawatts of AI computing capacity in the United States, achieving a goal it originally targeted for 2029. The company announced the milestone Wednesday, saying it added over 3GW in the past 90 days alone. Stargate, the joint venture with Oracle and SoftBank, has evolved from a $500B initiative into the umbrella brand for all of OpenAI's data center expansion plans.

¶ 01 WHY · IT · MATTERS

The accelerated timeline reflects intensifying competition for AI infrastructure as the company prepares for next-generation model releases. This milestone gives OpenAI significant leverage in the infrastructure race against Google, Microsoft, and others. The 10GW capacity is equivalent to powering roughly 3 million US homes.

¶ 02 WHY · IT · MATTERS

This milestone signals that the AI infrastructure race is accelerating faster than anticipated. With 10GW secured years ahead of schedule, OpenAI has secured a meaningful lead in physical compute capacity. This could be crucial for training the next generation of frontier models.

¶ 03 WHAT · TO · WATCH

Monitor for announcements on where the new capacity will be located. Watch for competitor responses from Microsoft, Google, and Amazon. Track whether this accelerates OpenAI's timeline for GPT-5 or successor models. The 3GW added in 90 days suggests aggressive expansion continues.
