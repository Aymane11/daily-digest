Google expands Pentagon AI access after Anthropic refusal

DoD gains unrestricted access for classified networks

BY · TECHCRUNCH · 3 MIN READ

★ WHAT HAPPENED

Google has granted the U.S. Department of Defense access to its AI for classified networks, allowing essentially all lawful uses. The deal follows Anthropic's public refusal to grant similar terms, after which the DoD branded Anthropic a "supply-chain risk." OpenAI and xAI signed deals immediately after Anthropic's refusal. 950 Google employees have signed an open letter asking the company to follow Anthropic's lead.

¶ 01 WHY · IT · MATTERS

This marks the third AI company to capitalize on Anthropic's principled stand. Google's deal includes language saying it doesn't intend for its AI to be used for domestic mass surveillance or autonomous weapons, similar to OpenAI's contract. However, it's unclear whether such provisions are legally binding. The DoD is currently in litigation with Anthropic over the supply-chain risk designation.

¶ 02 WHAT · TO · WATCH

Monitor the Anthropic vs. Pentagon lawsuit progress. A judge granted Anthropic an injunction against the designation while the case proceeds. Also watch for employee fallout at Google and whether more companies sign similar deals or follow Anthropic's model.
