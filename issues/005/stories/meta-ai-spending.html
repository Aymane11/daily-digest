Meta raises capex guidance to $125-145B for AI infrastructure

Company cites higher memory pricing and additional data center needs

BY · PYMNTS · 3 MIN READ

★ WHAT HAPPENED

Meta has raised its full-year capital expenditure guidance to $125-145B, up from $115-135B, citing higher memory pricing and additional data center capacity needs. The company acknowledged it has "consistently underestimated" its compute needs. More than 8M advertisers now use at least one of Meta's AI creative tools, with video generation tool users seeing 3%+ higher conversion rates.

¶ 01 WHY · IT · MATTERS

Meta's recommendation systems now train on longer user histories, processing richer signals across video, text, and interactions. Same-day posts now represent over 30% of recommended Reels on Facebook and Instagram, more than double the share from a year ago. Total video time on Facebook grew over 8% globally in Q1, the largest quarterly gain in four years.

¶ 02 WHAT · TO · WATCH

Review your ad strategy to incorporate Meta's AI creative tools. The 3% conversion lift from video generation tools is significant. Also watch for increasing competition for AI ad revenue as Google and others push harder into the space.
