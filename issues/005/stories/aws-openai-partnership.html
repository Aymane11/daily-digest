PARTNERSHIP CLOUD

AWS brings OpenAI models to Bedrock via expanded partnership

Latest models and Codex coding agent available in limited preview

BY · AMAZON · 3 MIN READ

★ WHAT HAPPENED

AWS announced a major expansion of its partnership with OpenAI, bringing the latest OpenAI models and Codex coding agent to Amazon Bedrock in limited preview. The partnership also includes Amazon Bedrock Managed Agents, enabling enterprises to build production-ready OpenAI-powered agents on AWS infrastructure. This comes one day after OpenAI and Microsoft revised their exclusivity terms.

¶ 01 WHY · IT · MATTERS

The deal marks a significant shift in OpenAI's distribution strategy, breaking exclusive reliance on Microsoft Azure. Enterprises can now access OpenAI models through the same APIs and security controls they use for other Bedrock models. The move also strengthens AWS's position against Azure in the frontier AI race.

¶ 02 WHAT · TO · WATCH

Evaluate whether your team can benefit from unified AI infrastructure through Bedrock. The managed agents feature could reduce development time for production AI applications. Watch for pricing details as the preview expands to general availability.
