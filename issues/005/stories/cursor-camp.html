Cursor Camp: Interactive guide to master AI-assisted coding

Free interactive course from the maker of Popcorn

BY · HACKER NEWS · 2 MIN READ

★ WHAT HAPPENED

Cursor Camp is a free interactive course that teaches developers how to effectively use Cursor, the AI-powered code editor. Created by Neal Agarwal (creator of Neal.fun and Popcorn), the course garnered 850+ points on Hacker News.

¶ 01 WHY · IT · MATTERS

As AI coding assistants become essential, understanding their full capabilities is crucial. This free course provides structured learning for developers looking to improve their AI-assisted workflow.

¶ 02 WHAT · TO · WATCH

Check out Cursor Camp if you use or want to use Cursor. The interactive format makes it easier to learn than reading documentation. Share with team members who are new to AI-assisted coding.
