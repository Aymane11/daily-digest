Anthropic in talks for $50B round at $900B valuation

Company received preemptive offers to double February valuation

BY · TECHCRUNCH · 3 MIN READ

★ WHAT HAPPENED

Anthropic is in discussions to raise a $40-50B funding round at an $850-900B valuation, more than doubling its $380B valuation from February. Sources say the company received multiple preemptive offers from investors eager to participate. The round could be its final private funding before a potential IPO. A board decision is expected in May.

¶ 01 WHY · IT · MATTERS

At $900B, Anthropic would match or surpass OpenAI's $852B valuation from its February $122B round. The company has shown dramatic revenue growth, with annual run rate surging from $9B at end of 2025 to $30-40B currently. One institutional investor prepared to commit as much as $5B has yet to secure a meeting with CFO Krishna Rao.

¶ 02 WHAT · TO · WATCH

Check whether Anthropic accepts the full $50B or opts for a smaller round. The terms will signal investor appetite for frontier AI labs ahead of potential public market debuts. Also watch for any board decisions on governance changes that often accompany mega-rounds.
