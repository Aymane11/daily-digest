Zig project maintains firm anti-AI contribution policy

No AI-generated code accepted, even with human review

BY · HACKER NEWS · 2 MIN READ

★ WHAT HAPPENED

The Zig programming language project has published its rationale for maintaining a firm anti-AI contribution policy. The project does not accept AI-generated code, even with human review. The policy has sparked discussion on Hacker News with 220+ points.

¶ 01 WHY · IT · MATTERS

Zig is one of the few projects with a clear, principled stance on AI-generated contributions. The rationale provides a template for other open source projects navigating similar questions.
