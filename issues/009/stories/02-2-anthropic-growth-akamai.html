01 CONTEXT

Anthropic's growth trajectory has reached a scale that separates it from every other AI company. While Block laid off 40%, Coinbase 14%, and Cloudflare 20% in recent weeks citing "AI readiness," Anthropic is hiring and expanding at a pace that makes it the fastest-growing company in tech history.

Anthropic signed a $1.8B AI cloud deal with Akamai on May 11 for computing capacity to meet surging demand. The deal sent Akamai shares up 25%. This comes on top of the SpaceX/Colossus data center deal, the Anthropic/Blackstone enterprise AI services company, and OpenAI-like growth metrics. Anthropic's capacity constraints have been so severe that developer relations reportedly suffered — with some developers receiving "dumber" model responses during peak loads.

02 WHAT'S NEW

The growth isn't just from API usage. Claude Code, Anthropic's coding agent, has seen massive enterprise adoption. Amazon gave the green light for Claude Code and Codex on Bedrock. Anthropic's higher usage limits, combined with the compute deal with SpaceX, signal that the company is solving its capacity bottleneck at remarkable speed and cost.
