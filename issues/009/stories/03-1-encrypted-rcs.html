Apple brings end-to-end encrypted RCS to iPhone in iOS 26.5 beta

iOS 26.5 adds end-to-end encrypted RCS messaging in beta — green bubble chats with Android users are now encrypted

BY · EDITORIAL TEAM · 2 MIN

★ WHAT HAPPENED

iOS 26.5, now in beta, adds end-to-end encrypted RCS messaging between iPhone and Android users. A lock icon on encrypted chats signals the conversation is secure. The feature is initially limited to a subset of carriers as Apple rolls out support gradually.

¶ 01 WHY · IT · MATTERS

This is the first time iPhone-to-Android messaging has had end-to-end encryption built in at the protocol level. It eliminates a key privacy gap that has existed since SMS. For developers building messaging apps, this reduces the pressure to build cross-platform chat features — the OS now provides what iMessage already offered, extended to Android.

¶ 02 WHAT · TO · WATCH

Check whether your carrier supports encrypted RCS in the iOS 26.5 beta. Enable the feature in Settings > Messages > RCS. If you build messaging products, evaluate whether encrypted RCS reduces your users' demand for third-party messaging apps. Watch for Google's response — Android already supports encrypted RCS and may add new features in response.
