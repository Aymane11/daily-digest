05 WHAT IT MEANS
3 TAKEAWAYS · LINKS
01

Long-context is no longer a closed-model advantage

The 10x KV cache reduction means running 1M-token sequences is genuinely practical on a single 8xB200 node. This brings codebase-scale reasoning and long-document analysis within reach for teams that need to self-host.

02

V4-Flash resets the cost floor for high-volume inference

At $0.14/M input tokens, V4-Flash costs less than GPT-5.4 Nano. For teams processing millions of documents or running agentic loops with many short calls, this is a material budget change.

03

The open/closed performance gap is closing faster than expected

V4-Pro sits within a fraction of Claude Opus 4.6 on SWE-bench. Simon Willison's characterization of a 3-6 month lag is optimistic for DeepSeek — the real gap may now be narrower on specific task categories.
