05 WHAT IT MEANS
3 TAKEAWAYS · LINKS
01

Frontier training no longer requires a single co-located cluster

The 235x bandwidth reduction means inter-datacenter links that were previously unusable for large-scale training are now viable. Labs with geographically distributed hardware can now pool those resources without sacrificing model quality.

02

Hardware failures become a scheduling problem, not a training halt

The 88% goodput under high-failure conditions versus 27% for synchronous methods is not a marginal improvement — it is the difference between a training run that completes and one that crashes. Self-healing infrastructure changes the economics of large runs.

03

This changes who can train frontier-scale models

Organizations without a single massive datacenter — research institutions, mid-tier cloud providers, national labs — now have a credible path to running large distributed training jobs without the bandwidth infrastructure that only hyperscalers can afford.
