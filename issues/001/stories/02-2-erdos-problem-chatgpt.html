05 WHAT IT MEANS
3 TAKEAWAYS · LINKS
01

LLMs can escape local optima that human experts are stuck in

The key insight is not that GPT-5.4 is smarter than Terence Tao — it is not. The insight is that it has no prior commitment to the path that experts had normalized over 60 years. That path-independence is a genuine capability that human mathematicians do not have.

02

The bottleneck shifts from idea generation to verification

Price's prompt produced an approach; Tao and Lichtman's months of work turned it into a proof. The division of labor — model for exploration, human expert for rigor — is a template for AI-assisted research going forward.

03

Amateur access to frontier tools changes who can do research

Price had no institutional affiliation and no formal training. ChatGPT Pro costs $200/month. The prerequisite for contributing to hard open problems is no longer a PhD or a research position.
