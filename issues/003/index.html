INDUSTRY M&A PARTNERSHIPS

Microsoft and OpenAI dissolve their exclusive partnership—the deal that built the AI industry as we know it

→ The 2019 exclusive is replaced by a preferred-provider arrangement expiring 2032. OpenAI can now close deals with AWS, Google Cloud, and others. Microsoft retains perpetual rights to GPT-4-era models.

OpenAI and Microsoft announced on April 27, 2026 that their exclusive partnership—the $13 billion deal struck in 2019 that made Microsoft the sole commercial cloud partner for OpenAI’s models—will be restructured as a non-exclusive preferred-provider arrangement expiring in 2032. OpenAI is simultaneously closing a $50 billion cloud infrastructure deal with AWS and entering discussions with Google Cloud. Microsoft retains perpetual rights to GPT-4-era model weights and a 49% revenue share from OpenAI’s enterprise API business. The restructuring ends a commercial constraint that had prevented OpenAI from closing deals with competitors since 2019—and signals that the company is preparing for a post-IPO operational model with diversified cloud infrastructure.
